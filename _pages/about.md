I am currently a sophomore undergraduate at **NYU Shanghai**, pursuing a double major in **Computer Science** and **Mathematics**.

My interest in research sits at the intersection of **Human-Computer Interaction**, **Machine Learning**, **Algorithms**, and **Computational Cultural Analytics**. I am broadly interested in leveraging quantitative algorithms and data-driven methods to model human creative practices, design interactive AI tools, and advance **Human-AI Complementarity**.

### Research & Interests

* **AI in Education & Risk-Aware Learning Systems:** Investigating the deployment of AI in educational contexts, focusing on strategies to **maximize pedagogical benefits and system performance while minimizing potential risks** (e.g., cognitive over-reliance, safety, and learning misalignment).
* **Domain-Specific Human Augmentation:** Applying algorithms and machine learning to amplify human potential and leverage domain expertise across diverse fields—including **arts, music, dance, healthcare, and environmental sciences**—focusing on human-AI complementarity rather than automation.
* **Bridging Qualitative & Quantitative Methods in HCI:** Developing computational and quantitative frameworks to model and empower qualitative user study insights. My goal is not to replace qualitative richness, but to use quantitative rigor to faithfully capture, scale, and represent qualitative findings in human-centered AI evaluation.


### Current Status

**Relevant Coursework:** 
* **Math & Computer Science:** Machine Learning, Algorithms, Data Structures, Probability and Statistics, Multivariable Calculus, Linear Algebra, Foundations of Mathematical Methods.
* **Arts & Movement:** Contemporary Dance, Intermediate Ballet. *(Providing a domain-specific foundation for computational cultural analytics).*

Recently, I co-authored a paper submitted to **CHI 2027 (2nd author)** titled *"When Assistance Becomes Risk: Human Verification and Research Decisions with Generative AI."* Based on an empirical user study of researchers interacting with AI-assisted workflows, our work investigates how humans evaluate Generative AI outputs, the evidence they rely on, and the potential risks of automation bias. We argue for HCI interfaces that make human verification and decision-making a central design priority. 

Moving forward, my goal is to bridge my rigorous quantitative/CS foundation with user-centered design to build intelligent, domain-aware interactive systems. I aim to design AI tools that ensure safe, complementary Human-AI collaboration—amplifying human expertise while mitigating the risks of over-reliance.
